RepGrind – phone guide

RepGrind counts your reps, runs the workout clock and puts the whole class on one leaderboard. Everything works in the browser, without an account, and offline once it's open.

Getting started

  1. Open rextherunt77.github.io/repgrind on your phone.
  2. Add it to your home screen: in Chrome Install app, in Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen like an app.
  3. The app opens on the WOD tab, ready to count a For time workout. Tap the big button for each rep; the first rep starts the clock.

The four tabs

TabWhat it's for
WODCounting during the workout: the count, the clock, the big button
ResultsLeaderboard, rep chart, analysis and export after the workout
HistoryEarlier workouts, one row each
SetupThe workout, Count by, athletes and WOD Code

While a workout runs, a pill at the top of every tab shows the clock; tap it to go back to WOD.

Counting on the WOD tab

  • The big button adds a rep, or a whole set or round (see Count by). On the WOD tab it fills the lower part of the screen, so a palm anywhere there counts.
  • −1 REP / Undo set takes back the last press. +1 rep adds a single rep.
  • Tap mode turns the whole screen into the button: put the phone on the floor and slap it.
  • Start gives a 3-2-1 countdown. Pause, Stop and Reset are at the top.
  • The chips at the top switch between athletes.

Setup: the workout

WorkoutWhat it does
For timeCounts up; with a target it stops when the reps are done. "1 round = … reps" counts rounds, e.g. 5 rounds of 30
AMRAPCounts down the time cap; with "1 round = … reps" it shows rounds + reps
EMOMA new interval every minute, with an optional target per interval
ChipperCounts down from the total reps
Rep schemeSets like 21-15-9 for each movement. Tap movements in the cloud instead of typing them
IntervalsWork and rest periods, e.g. Tabata 8 × 20 s / 10 s

Count by rep, set or round

One press can count a rep, finish the current set ("Set done · +21") or a whole round. A rep scheme counts by set by default, For time and AMRAP with a round size by round. A dimmed choice says what it needs when you tap it. A second press within a second is ignored, so a bouncing palm can't finish two sets.

Athletes

Add an athlete or team with Add athlete on Setup. Each gets a chip on the WOD tab, their own count and their own line in the rep chart.

WOD Code: several phones in one workout

  • Coach: on Setup tap Create WOD Code and share the invite link.
  • Athlete: open the link, type your name and tap Join as athlete. Your phone becomes your rep button and your reps appear on the coach's phone.
  • TV or tablet: open the link and tap Join as screen. It shows the live leaderboard.

Joining needs the internet for a moment; after that the phones talk directly. Up to 8 athletes; everyone should run the same version.

Big screen and the rep chart

The screen button at the top opens a full-screen leaderboard for a TV. It switches between Ranking and Rep chart: total reps over time, one line per athlete, with every change of leader ringed. Touch or drag along a rep chart to see the time and everyone's reps at that moment.

Results and export

After the workout the Results tab opens by itself: leaderboard, rep chart, reps per minute, analysis and sets. Export copies a result for WodConnect or saves a CSV, TXT or TCX file for Garmin Connect and Strava. New WOD saves the workout to History and goes back to Setup.

History

One row per workout with the date, the workout and everyone's reps and time. Tap a row for its results, rep chart and Export this workout. Clear history removes everything.

Settings

Behind the gear: language, sounds and vibration, voice counting, the tap-mode gap, What's new, Help, Show the tour again, the classic one-page layout, anonymous usage counts and backup.

Good to know

  • Updates: when a new version is out, a banner offers Update. It never shows during a workout.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Use Download backup in Settings before clearing the browser's data.
  • Something not working? Settings has a link to the previous version (2.5.0); it uses the same saved workouts.
